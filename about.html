About

Hi, I’m Luke, a woodworker and designer based in Cambridgeshire. I create bespoke woodwork for homes and gardens, from the first idea and design concept through to the finished build.

With over 5 years’ experience and a contemporary understanding of interior and exterior design, I help turn ideas into practical, well-made wooden features.

My work includes built-in cabinets, bookcases, desks, table tops, gates and garden buildings.

Whether you already have a clear idea or need advice on how to use an awkward space, I’d be happy to talk it through.
