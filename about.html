About

Hi, I’m Luke, a woodworker and designer based in Cambridgeshire. I have a creative approach to my work and enjoy taking on bespoke projects, starting with the design and concept through to the build. With a contemporary understanding of interior and exterior design and over 5 years of experience, I can turn ideas for your home and garden into tangible woodworking projects. Some of my work includes built-in cabinets, bookcases, desks, table tops, gates, and garden buildings.

Whether you already have an idea of what you want or just need some advice on how to use that awkward space in your home, feel free to get in touch via the contact page.
